This assistant uses an offline guidance library, not a live AI diagnosis system. For exact pesticide or fertilizer recommendations, confirm the crop, pest/disease and local label directions with your agriculture officer or KVK.

How to get a more accurate answer

  • Write the crop name and its growth stage.
  • Describe where the symptom appears: older leaves, new leaves, stem, roots or fruit.
  • Mention when it started and whether the whole field or only a few plants are affected.
  • Do not mix or spray pesticides based only on a guess.
